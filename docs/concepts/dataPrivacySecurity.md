## Where your data views are kept

Your data views are saved in your browser, on the device you are using. They are not sent to DPUse's servers, so they are not backed up and do not follow you to another device or browser.

- **Each account has its own data views.** When you sign in, you see only the data views saved under your account in this browser. Signing out does not delete them, so they are there when you sign back in.
- **Using DPUse without signing in** saves data views to a guest area of the browser. Anyone who uses DPUse in the same browser without signing in sees the same guest data views. When you sign in after working as a guest, DPUse offers to move your guest data views into your account.
- **Separate is not the same as secure.** Accounts keep their data views apart, but they are not encrypted, and anyone with access to the browser's storage can read them. A data view can include sample rows from your data.
- **On a shared computer**, choose **Sign out and remove my data** from the session menu. This removes your data views from the browser before signing you out. It cannot be undone.

Saving data views to your account on DPUse's servers is planned. Until then, the browser holds the only copy.
